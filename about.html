About Yishog Integrated Services

Over 15 years of industrial belt expertise — serving manufacturers across Nigeria and West Africa with precision, integrity and commitment.

Our Story

A Trusted Name in Industrial Transmission

Yishog Integrated Services Limited was established to fill a critical gap in the Nigerian industrial market — providing manufacturers and engineers with reliable, high-quality transmission belts and hoses sourced from globally recognised manufacturers.

Over the past 15+ years we have grown to supply clients across the aluminium, automotive, textile, food processing, construction and many other sectors, building a reputation that rests on a single uncompromising standard: quality.

"There might be compromise on price, in Yishog there will never be compromise on quality."
