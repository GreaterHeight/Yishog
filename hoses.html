Industrial Hoses

Steam hoses, air hoses and high-pressure hoses for industrial fluid transfer — EPDM, NBR, PTFE and braided constructions.

Steam Hoses

Steam hoses are designed for the conveyance of saturated and superheated steam in industrial applications. Constructed with an EPDM inner tube, high-tensile wire braid reinforcement and a heat-resistant outer cover, they withstand continuous steam service at working pressures up to 18 bar.

  • Inner tube: EPDM (steam and condensate resistant)
  • Reinforcement: 2–4 wire braid
  • Working pressure up to 18 bar / 260 psi
  • Temperature range: -40°C to +210°C
  • Standards: EN ISO 6134 Type 2

Air Hoses

Air hoses supply compressed air to pneumatic tools, equipment and cylinders. Available in textile-braid and wire-braid constructions across a range of bore sizes from 6mm to 51mm. Nitrile (NBR) inner tube resists oil contamination in compressed air lines.

  • Inner tube: NBR (oil-mist resistant)
  • Reinforcement: textile or wire braid
  • Working pressure: 10–40 bar depending on size
  • Temperature range: -20°C to +80°C
  • Standards: EN 559 / ISO 2398

Pressure Hoses

High-pressure hydraulic and water hoses for demanding fluid transfer applications. Two- and four-wire braid and spiral-wound constructions are available to handle working pressures from 100 bar to over 700 bar for high-pressure water jetting and hydraulic systems.

  • 1-wire, 2-wire, 4-wire braid available
  • 4-spiral and 6-spiral for ultra-high pressure
  • Working pressure range: 100–700+ bar
  • SAE 100R / EN 853 / EN 856 standards
  • Hydraulic oil, water and mild chemical service
