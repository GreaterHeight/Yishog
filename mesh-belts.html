Mesh Belts

Open-weave metal and synthetic mesh belts for ventilation, drainage and heat-transfer applications in food, glass and industrial processes.

Balanced Spiral Mesh Belts

Balanced spiral mesh belts are constructed from alternating left- and right-hand coils linked by straight cross-rods. This construction produces a belt with a balanced (symmetric) weave, uniform aperture and excellent stability under lateral tension.

  • Uniform aperture for consistent airflow/drainage
  • Carbon steel, stainless steel, galvanised options
  • Width up to 4,000mm
  • Applications: baking, drying, cooling, washing

Compound Balanced Mesh Belts

Compound balanced mesh adds additional intermediate spirals to increase the number of coils per unit width. The result is a finer, denser mesh suitable for carrying smaller products or where lower surface opening ratios are required.

  • Finer mesh for smaller product support
  • Higher strength per unit width
  • Pitch and wire gauge matched to load
  • Food-grade stainless steel options
