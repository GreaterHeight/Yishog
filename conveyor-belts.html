Conveyor Belts

Industrial flat and modular conveyor belts for continuous material transport — rubber, PVC, PU and speciality compounds.

Flat Conveyor Belts

Flat conveyor belts are the backbone of material handling in manufacturing, logistics and food processing. We supply rubber, PVC and PU flat belts in a range of grades including general-purpose, oil-resistant, heat-resistant and food-safe compounds.

  • General purpose, oil-resistant, heat-resistant grades
  • FDA-approved food-grade compounds available
  • Cover grades EP, NN, AA, AB, BB, CC
  • Custom lengths and widths available

Cleated & Chevron Conveyor Belts

Cleated and chevron-profile belts are designed for inclined conveying where standard flat belts would allow product to slide. The profiled surface provides grip to carry bulk materials, packages and aggregates at inclines up to 35° or more depending on cleat height.

  • Cleat heights from 10mm to 50mm+
  • Chevron patterns for loose/bulk materials
  • Suitable for inclines up to 35–40°
  • Rubber and PVC base compounds
