Vee Belts

Classical and narrow V-belts (A, B, C, D, E sections) for industrial, agricultural and HVAC drive applications.

Classical V-Belts

Classical V-belts (also called Vee belts) are the most widely used power transmission belt. The trapezoidal cross-section wedges into the pulley groove, creating friction drive. Available in A, B, C, D and E sections to ISO 22 / DIN 2215 standards.

  • Sections: A, B, C, D, E
  • Compliant with ISO 22 / DIN 2215
  • Oil-resistant and heat-resistant grades available
  • Matched sets available for multi-belt drives

Wrapped & Raw Edge V-Belts

Wrapped (standard) V-belts have a fabric cover over the entire cross-section. Raw-edge V-belts expose the rubber compound at the sides, improving flexibility and grip — particularly at small pulley diameters. Raw-edge cogged variants further reduce bending stress.

  • Wrapped construction for general use
  • Raw-edge for higher efficiency and flexibility
  • Cogged raw-edge for small-pulley applications
  • Suitable for agricultural, HVAC, industrial drives
